About Camp Adam Fisher

The Southeast's largest and oldest all-volunteer camp for kids with Type 1 diabetes — proudly celebrating 58 years.

Our Story

Born from love in 1968.

"

It's a fact of life for kids with diabetes — they face a set of unique challenges. Which is why we think they deserve a set of unique opportunities. That's the big idea behind Camp Adam Fisher, an annual summer camp born in 1968 out of a father's wish for his daughter.


Our mission is to offer all the experiences, lessons and benefits of summer camp to children with Type 1 diabetes, while teaching them how to live longer and healthier lives. Our pledge is to do so in a safe, well-monitored environment where professional attention can be afforded to each camper's individual needs.


It's simple really. A week of camp, packed with great activities, fun, friendship and self-discovery.

The Location

110 acres of pure adventure.

Camp Adam Fisher is held at the R.M. Cooper 4-H Leadership Center, located on a 110-acre peninsula on the Wyboo Creek section of Lake Marion in Summerton, S.C. — only 15 minutes from Interstate 95.

Address: 8001 M W Rickenbaker Rd, Summerton, South Carolina 29148
